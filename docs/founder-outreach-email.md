# Founder outreach: selling a shut-down company's data

For the leads in `data-leads-shutdowns-2026-10.csv`. Send one at a time, personalised, from your own address. Not a mass mailing.

## Rules (CAN-SPAM, and not getting blocked)

- An honest subject line. No "Re:" or "Fwd:" on a first email.
- Say who you are and that you earn a referral fee.
- A real postal address in the footer (a PO box works).
- A one-line opt-out. Honour it within 10 days and never email that person again.
- Only addresses the person published for contact (company site, their own posts). No scraping, no guessed addresses.
- Send at most ~20 a day, and only from a domain you're happy to have rate-limited. Use a separate sending domain if you're unsure.
- Send one follow-up at most, then stop.

## Email: company winding down (best timing)

**Subject:** {Company}'s data before the {date} shutdown

Hi {first name},

Sorry to see {Company} is winding down. I saw {where you saw it: their post / the article}.

One thing worth checking before the servers go dark: AI labs are now paying for the internal records of real companies (Slack history, tickets, docs and code, with personal data stripped out). Reported deals run from about $10K for small startup archives to $10M for Spirit Airlines' data, and micro1 says approved packages earn $100K to $2M+. With {headcount} people over {years} years, {Company} is in the range buyers look at{, and Mercor is specifically buying {industry} data right now}.

I run Artificial Jobs, which tracks these deals: https://www.artificialjobs.dev/sell-company-data/what-is-it-worth. If it's useful, I can tell you which buyers fit and introduce you. If a deal closes, the buyer program pays me a referral fee.

Worth a 15-minute call before {date}?

{Your name}
Artificial Jobs · {postal address}
If you'd rather not hear from me again, just reply "no" and I won't.

## Variant: already shut down

Swap the first two paragraphs for:

> I saw {Company} shut down in {month}. Sorry, that's a hard call. If the company still has its Slack, tickets, docs or code archived, those records may be worth real money now: AI labs are buying the internal data of defunct companies (personal data stripped out). Reported deals run from about $10K for small startup archives to $10M for Spirit Airlines' data.

Add: "Whoever controls the company's assets now (you, the board, or a liquidator) would need to approve a sale."

## Variant: insolvency administrator (e.g. Orbex)

Address the administrator. Frame it as asset recovery for creditors: "an asset class that often isn't on the schedule: the company's internal records, which AI labs now buy." Keep the same disclosure and opt-out.

## Follow-up (once, 5 to 7 days later)

**Subject:** Re: {Company}'s data before the {date} shutdown

Hi {first name}, a quick nudge in case this got buried. Happy to send the list of buyers that fit {Company} by email if a call is too much right now. Either way, good luck with what's next.

## Before you send

- Read the row's caveats. Some companies' customer data isn't theirs to sell, and some teams were acqui-hired with their IP.
- Lead with internal operating data only. Never suggest selling customer or user data.
- Check micro1's referral terms allow direct outreach (not yet checked).
